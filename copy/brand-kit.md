## 3. Positioning

SILAVU is a fine-jewellery house built on one signature: the SILAVU knot, two
interlaced loops held by two open arcs. Every piece carries that line, in
18K gold set with hand-matched diamonds. The house sits beside the grand
Paris maisons in craft and finish, but feels more personal: you don't buy
from a shelf. You book a private viewing, and a concierge brings the pieces
to you. It works from Tel Aviv and Dubai.

## 4. Target audience

Women aged 28 to 55 in Israel and the Gulf who buy fine jewellery for
themselves, mark a milestone (an engagement, an anniversary, a birth), or
receive it as a considered gift. They know Cartier and Messika, want
something less common, and expect to be served personally. They read in
Hebrew, English, French, Arabic or Russian. [placeholder: owner to confirm]

## 5. Brand personality

Luminous, intimate, assured, sculptural, quiet, precise, feminine and modern.

SILAVU speaks softly and lets the stones do the talking. It never shouts a
discount and never explains too much.

## 14. Landing page sections

Rebuilt in Phase 5 on the owner's one concrete reference: "organised, high
level, and when you scroll things move like in test3". It uses test3's
language (black and white chapters, light uppercase Jost with the second half
of each line in grey, curved waves between chapters) on the organised house
structure.

1. Announcement bar and header: Menu, links, the logo centred,
   Appointment and the wish list.
2. **The line of desire:** the LUMIÈRE film, with the words on its white side.
   As you scroll, the film grows a little and the words rise away.
3. **The signature:** test3's own drawing. One line writes "Silavu" by hand
   across a black-and-white diagonal, exactly as far as the page has moved,
   with the dot of the i and the sparks at the end.
4. **Fine jewellery, made to be lived in:** the collection grid, with filters,
   wish list, quick view and an appointment card.
5. **The box:** held on screen while the scroll opens it. Its three lines come

6. **Up close:** the three on-model photographs (ICON, SOUL, MOMENT). They

7. **Light goes in. Fire comes out:** loose stones on black leather drift

8. **PROMISE and BLUSH.**

## 17. Design bans

Phase 4 obeys these and Phase 5 adds to them.

- No cream or off-white page background.
- No italic accent words inside headlines.
- No numbered "01 / 02 / 03" section labels.
- No monospace eyebrow labels.
- No pill-shaped buttons.
- No gradient-filled headline text.
- No emoji as icons.
- No three identical feature cards in a row.
- No purple-to-blue gradients.
- No glass effect on every card.
- From the owner's earlier feedback:
  - nothing laggy, which means no heavy scroll-frame sequences on phones;
  - no rendered pendant that looks fake;
  - the logo is never mirrored.
- No dark or black page; the owner wants white (Phase 5). Cream stays banned
  too: the white is neutral.
- No generated or rendered imagery on the page; only the house's own films and
  photographs (Phase 5).
- Added in Phase 4's design check:
  - no on-model photograph cropped so the lips are the subject (crop to the
    jewellery);
  - no line art behind body text where it cuts through the letters;
  - no transparent product cut-out with a leftover white backdrop.

## 18. Experience

Changed in Phase 5. The owner rejected the dark scroll-film site and asked
to start over in white.

- **The opening:** the house film "The Choice" (white marble, a hand lifts
  SOUL, SOUL worn with a white shirt) as a muted loop. Below it on white sit
  the title SOUL and two calls to action.
- **The second film:** LUMIÈRE (the SOUL macro, then worn with a black dress),
  tall, beside its words.
- **How films play:**
  - The right cut for the screen: 16:9 on desktop, 9:16 on phones, at about
    1 MB each.
  - Each film loads only when it is near the screen.
  - Only the film in view plays.
- **Scroll effects** (Phase 5, after "not effective when you scroll"):
  - the opening film recedes into a frame as SOUL rises below it;
  - the intro sentence lights word by word;
  - pictures drift and open like a curtain;
  - the collection slides sideways while pinned;
  - the LUMIÈRE window opens to the whole screen;
  - the house name moves across;
  - desktop wheel scrolling is smoothed with Lenis.

  Only transform, opacity and clip-path change, and only for the effect on
  screen. No 3D and no frame sequences.
- The generated velvet hero, film and images from Phases 2 and 3 are kept in
  `assets/` but not used.


## 19. The vitrine (`vitrine.html`)

Built on the owner's reference video: a dark wall of lit niches, one piece in
each.

- **The wall:** seven niches carved in honed basalt, each with a lamp in its
  ceiling that draws a scallop of light on the back wall and a polished black
  slab for a ledge. The owner asked for it to be "more realistic" and chose
  free renders now and photographed niches later. So the niches are rendered
  in Blender (`website/tools/niche-blender.py`), an exception to the ban on
  rendered imagery that covers the setting only.
- **The light:** each lamp carries a faint tint of its piece (cool for white
  gold, warm for yellow, pink for BLUSH).
- **The pieces:** always the house's own photographs, set on the ledge with a
  contact shadow, a top-lit shade and a reflection in the slab.
- **Later:** photographed niches, one per piece, once Higgsfield credits are
  topped up. Each spend is priced first and waits for the owner's "go".
