## 3. Positioning

SILAVU is a fine-jewellery house built on one signature: the SILAVU knot, two
interlaced loops held by two open arcs. Every piece carries that line, in
18K gold set with hand-matched diamonds. The house sits beside the grand
Paris maisons in craft and finish, but feels more personal: you don't buy
from a shelf. You book a private viewing, and a concierge brings the pieces
to you. It works from Tel Aviv and Dubai.

## 4. Target audience

Women aged 28 to 55 in Israel and the Gulf who buy fine jewellery for
themselves, mark a milestone (an engagement, an anniversary, a birth), or
receive it as a considered gift. They know Cartier and Messika, want
something less common, and expect to be served personally. They read in
Hebrew, English, French, Arabic or Russian. [placeholder: owner to confirm]

## 5. Brand personality

Luminous, intimate, assured, sculptural, quiet, precise, feminine and modern.

SILAVU speaks softly and lets the stones do the talking. It never shouts a
discount and never explains too much.

- Blush is used sparingly: the call to action, active states, the rim light

## 7. Typography

- **Headings: Bellefair** (Google Fonts, one weight). It is a high-contrast
  display serif with real character, and it has Hebrew glyphs, so English and
  Hebrew headlines share one voice.
- **Body: Assistant** (Google Fonts, 300 / 400 / 600). It is a clean humanist
  sans with Latin and Hebrew.
- Fallbacks for Arabic and Russian:
  - Noto Naskh Arabic for Arabic headings and body.
  - Cormorant (already self-hosted) for Cyrillic headings and Assistant's
    system fallback for Cyrillic body.
- Uppercase labels are tracked at 0.18em. Hebrew and Arabic are never
  tracked.

## 10. Key benefits

- **One signature line.** The knot runs through every piece, so a collection
  grows as one family.
- **Hand-matched diamonds.** Every brilliant in a pavé is matched by eye for
  size and fire, then hand-set.
- **Signed by the house.** An engraved SILAVU tag or inner-band signature on
  every piece.
- **A private viewing.** In the Tel Aviv or Dubai salon, or at home, with a
  concierge.
- **Care for life.** Free cleaning, polishing and claw checks for as long as
  you own the piece. [placeholder]
- **Certified stones.** Each centre stone and pavé lot comes with its
  certificate. [placeholder]

## 11. Visual mood

- **Light:** night. One hard spotlight from above and behind. A Blush rim
  light on one side and an Ice rim light on the other.
- **Surface:** black velvet or wet black obsidian that holds a soft reflection.
- **Atmosphere:** fine light dust drifting slowly through the beam, like
  diamond dust in the air. Tiny star-shaped sparks where the light hits the
  stones.
- **Camera:** macro lens, very shallow depth of field, slow and steady, never
  shaky.
- **Overall:** intimate, hushed, expensive.

## 17. Design bans

Phase 4 obeys these and Phase 5 adds to them.

- No cream or off-white page background.
- No italic accent words inside headlines.
- No numbered "01 / 02 / 03" section labels.
- No monospace eyebrow labels.
- No pill-shaped buttons.
- No gradient-filled headline text.
- No emoji as icons.
- No three identical feature cards in a row.
- No purple-to-blue gradients.
- No glass effect on every card.
- From the owner's earlier feedback:
  - nothing laggy, which means no heavy scroll-frame sequences on phones;
  - no rendered pendant that looks fake;
  - the logo is never mirrored.
