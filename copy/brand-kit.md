## 3. Positioning

SILAVU is a fine-jewellery house built on one signature: the SILAVU knot, two
interlaced loops held by two open arcs. Every piece carries that line, in
18K gold set with hand-matched diamonds. The house sits beside the grand
Paris maisons in craft and finish, but feels more personal: you don't buy
from a shelf. You book a private viewing, and a concierge brings the pieces
to you. It works from Tel Aviv and Dubai.

## 4. Target audience

Women aged 28 to 55 in Israel and the Gulf who buy fine jewellery for
themselves, mark a milestone (an engagement, an anniversary, a birth), or
receive it as a considered gift. They know Cartier and Messika, want
something less common, and expect to be served personally. They read in
Hebrew, English, French, Arabic or Russian. [placeholder: owner to confirm]

## 5. Brand personality

Luminous, intimate, assured, sculptural, quiet, precise, feminine and modern.

SILAVU speaks softly and lets the stones do the talking. It never shouts a
discount and never explains too much.

  grows as one family.

  every piece.
- **A private viewing.** In the Tel Aviv or Dubai salon, or at home, with a
  concierge.

## 17. Design bans

Phase 4 obeys these and Phase 5 adds to them.

- No cream or off-white page background.
- No italic accent words inside headlines.
- No numbered "01 / 02 / 03" section labels.
- No monospace eyebrow labels.
- No pill-shaped buttons.
- No gradient-filled headline text.
- No emoji as icons.
- No three identical feature cards in a row.
- No purple-to-blue gradients.
- No glass effect on every card.
- From the owner's earlier feedback:
  - nothing laggy, which means no heavy scroll-frame sequences on phones;
  - no rendered pendant that looks fake;
  - the logo is never mirrored.
- No dark or black page; the owner wants white (Phase 5). Cream stays banned
  too: the white is neutral.
- No generated or rendered imagery on the page; only the house's own films and
  photographs (Phase 5).
- Added in Phase 4's design check:
  - no on-model photograph cropped so the lips are the subject (crop to the
    jewellery);
  - no line art behind body text where it cuts through the letters;
  - no transparent product cut-out with a leftover white backdrop.

## 18. Experience

Changed in Phase 5. The owner rejected the dark scroll-film site and asked
to start over in white.

- **The opening:** the house film "The Choice" (white marble, a hand lifts
  SOUL, SOUL worn with a white shirt) as a muted loop. Below it on white sit
  the title SOUL and two calls to action.
- **The second film:** LUMIÈRE (the SOUL macro, then worn with a black dress),
  tall, beside its words.
- **How films play:**
  - The right cut for the screen: 16:9 on desktop, 9:16 on phones, at about
    1 MB each.
  - Each film loads only when it is near the screen.
  - Only the film in view plays.
- **Nothing scroll-driven and no 3D,** so the page never lags.
- The generated velvet hero, film and images from Phases 2 and 3 are kept in
  `assets/` but not used.
