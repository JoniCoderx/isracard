Carat, explained

Carat is weight, not size. The total is shared by thirty-six stones, and a stone's width grows with the cube root of its weight: double the carats and each stone is only about a quarter wider. Everything below updates as you choose.

05 · For clients

How it works, and what you get.

Nothing here happens in a shop. One conversation, and a person carries the rest.

Book a private viewingStart a bespoke piece
I
Private viewing, where you are
At your residence, your hotel, or our rooms in Dubai and Tel Aviv. Usually within forty-eight hours of your message.
II
Stones chosen under your light
Three to five certified stones brought to you. You compare them yourself, with a loupe, in daylight and at night.
III
Made by hand in Dubai
Four to six weeks for a Line or a bespoke piece. Every stone laser-inscribed with its report number and set by one setter.
IV
Delivered in person, insured
Worldwide, by a member of the house, with the GIA or IGI reports and a valuation for your insurer. In a black lacquer box with the mark in the lid.
V
Cared for, for life
Cleaning, prong checks, resizing and re-polishing, at no charge, for as long as you own the piece.
VI
Discretion, absolute
No shopfront, no mailing list, no photographs of clients. Your name stays between you and one concierge.
